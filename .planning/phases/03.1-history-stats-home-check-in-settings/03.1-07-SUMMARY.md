# Plan 03.1-07 Summary: SettingsBloc, Unit Conversion, General & Workout Sections, Sub-Sheets

## Overview
Implemented `SettingsBloc` (supporting all region-level settings mutations for Phase 3.1) and built the first half of the Settings screen: the local-data notice ('Your data'), the General section with bridged unit conversion and decimal/calendar toggles, the During a Workout section, the Workout Controls sheet, and the Effort Scale help sheet.

## Key Deliverables

1. **BLoC & DI (`lib/features/settings/bloc/`, `lib/app/providers/settings_providers.dart`)**:
   - `SettingsBloc`: single-writer path managing settings through `GymStateCubit.mutate` and `replaceState`.
     - `SettingsUnitSwitched`: offers full whole-state conversion via `UnitsJs.convertStateUnit` or label-only switch via `mutate((s) => s.copyWith(unit: v))`.
     - `SettingsGeneralChanged`: updates `wdec`, `weekStart`, and `checkIn`.
     - `SettingsWorkoutChanged`: updates `weighIn`, `workoutView`, `restSec`, `restPauseSec`, `keepAwake`, `gifSize`, `sound`, `soundOnSilent`, `timerFlash`, `effort` (cleans legacy `showRir` when effort is set).
     - `SettingsWorkoutControlsChanged`: merges toggles into `WorkoutControls` with existing keys preserved.
     - `SettingsBodyChanged`: sets body diagram preference.
     - `SettingsEquipFilterToggled`, `SettingsActiveEquipChanged`, `SettingsEquipProfileSaved`, `SettingsEquipProfileDeleted`: equipment profile mutations (for plan 03.1-10).
     - `SettingsResetConfirmed`: resets state to defaults via `overlayOnDefaults(const {})` and emits `SettingsResetDone` signal.
   - Registered `BlocProvider<SettingsBloc>` in `settingsBlocProviders`.

2. **Sections & UI (`lib/features/settings/view/sections/`)**:
   - **`general_section.dart`**:
     - 'Weight unit': Segmented kg/lb with modal sheet offering 'Convert the numbers' (`convert: true`) and 'Keep the numbers, change the label' (`convert: false`).
     - 'Weight decimals': Segmented 0.5 (1) / 0.25 (2).
     - 'Week starts on': Segmented Monday (1) / Sunday (0).
     - 'Gym check-in': Switch for home card visibility.
     - Footnote explaining conversion option.
   - **`workout_section.dart`**:
     - 'Weigh in before workouts' switch.
     - 'Workout view' segmented (Cards, List, Compact).
     - 'Workout controls' row opening sheet.
     - 'Rest timer' and 'Rest-pause rest' `SelectRow` pickers.
     - 'Exercise animations' segmented (Full, Small, Hidden).
     - 'Sounds' and 'Flash screen when timer ends' switches (values stored; hardware audio playback and screen flash deferred to Phase 5).
     - 'Effort per set' segmented (Off, RIR, RPE) with info button opening help sheet.
     - Screen wake lock and play-on-silent hidden per openGym platform-gating rules (Phase 5).

3. **Sub-Sheets (`lib/features/settings/view/sheets/`)**:
   - **`workout_controls_sheet.dart`**:
     - 4 switches: 'Weight and reps buttons' (`steppers`), 'Drop and burst shortcuts on every set' (`setShortcuts`), 'Superset buttons in the exercise header' (`pairButtons`), 'Move, swap and remove buttons below the exercise' (`exerciseButtons`).
   - **`effort_help_sheet.dart`**:
     - Complete RIR vs RPE table (0/10, 1/9, 2/8 highlighted as typical, 3/7, 4+/≤6) and descriptive fatigue guidance.

4. **Settings Screen (`lib/features/settings/view/settings_screen.dart`)**:
   - Top-level single scroll view with back chevron to `/home`.
   - 'Your data' card: 'All data stays on this phone' with lock icon.
   - General and Workout sections mounted with clear insertion point markers for Plan 03.1-10.
   - Listens to `SettingsResetDone` signal to return `/home` and display confirmation toast.
