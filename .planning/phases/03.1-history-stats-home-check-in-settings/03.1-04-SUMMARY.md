# Plan 03.1-04 Summary: ViewAggregatesJs Facade & effort.js Extensions

## Overview
Transcribed openGym's view-level aggregates into a JS shim (`tool/js/shims/view-aggregates-shim.js`) so that every number rendered across History, Stats, Home, Calendar, and WorkoutDetail is computed directly by QuickJS rather than hand-rolled in Dart (strictly upholding JS-04). Also bridged the remaining `effort.js` functions used by Stats' EffortCard, established 31 fixture cases with deterministic cross-checking, generated typed Dart models and facades, and verified full device parity.

## Key Deliverables

1. **JS Bridge & Shims**:
   - **`tool/js/shims/view-aggregates-shim.js`**:
     - `heatmapDays(S)`: computes 53-week training metrics (`n`, `vol`, `min`, `level`) and quartile thresholds (`t1`, `t2`, `t3`) matching `Heatmap.jsx:11-19`.
     - `statsTiles(S, nowMs, todayIso)`: computes aggregate workouts count, this month's count, and 30-day bodyweight delta matching `Stats.jsx:297-302`.
     - `bodyweightPoints(S, rangeDays, nowMs)`: generates filtered `[{t, y, d}]` chart points matching `Stats.jsx:297-298`.
     - `exerciseProgress(S, exId, kind, names)`: computes exercise history, current exercise metadata, 1RM/volume points, and effort points matching `Stats.jsx:305-435`.
     - `calendarMonth(S, y, mo)`: computes month workout count, total volume, and duration ms matching `sheets.jsx:1745-1786`.
     - `workoutDetailGroups(w)`: groups entries by routine id (`rid`), calculating per-group set count and volume matching `sheets.jsx:1666-1740`.
     - `bwGoal(S)`: calculates bodyweight target delta, goal reached flag, and gain/lose direction matching `Home.jsx` & `GoalSheet`.
     - `homeView(S, todayIso)`: returns bodyweight delta, week-to-date workout count, target workouts per week, 30-day bodyweight chart points, and goal info matching `Home.jsx`.
   - **`effort.js` extensions**:
     - Registered `hasEffort`, `displayScale`, `scaleName`, `avgRir`, `effortSummary`, `effortHistogram`, `isHardSet`, `MIN_RATED`, and `BUCKETS` in `INVENTORY`.
     - Banned timezone-sensitive `effort.effortWeeks` from fixtures.
   - Pinned bundle rebuild: regenerated `assets/js/opengym-lib.bundle.js` with `viewAgg` export.
