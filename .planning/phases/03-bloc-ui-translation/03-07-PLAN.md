| T-03-21 | Denial of Service | free-text name/notes | mitigate | Rename slices to 60 chars; notes slice to HistoryJs.noteMax, as openGym does |
| T-03-22 | Integrity | concurrent events | mitigate | Sequential transformer + single-writer mutate |
</threat_model>
